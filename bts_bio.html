Meet the Band!

김남준

Kim Namjoon, born in late 1994, is the visionary BTS leader and main rapper. From the moment he was the first member selected, his deep intellect, fluent English, and thoughtful leadership have steered the group's success.

RM is the one who sets the standard for the group's artistic direction and social messaging, often contributing deeply insightful lyrics. Known as the “God of Destruction” for his clumsiness, he balances his serious, philosophical side with a charming, approachable personality. He is the eloquent bridge between BTS and the rest of the world.

박지민

Park Jimin, born in 1995, is a main dancer and lead vocalist, celebrated for his breathtaking stage presence and captivating duality. Trained in contemporary dance, his movements are fluid, expressive, and deeply emotional. Jimin's vocal delivery is instantly recognizable, often hitting the highest notes in the group's discography.

Off stage, he is known for being incredibly sweet, caring, and affectionate toward his members and ARMY. His dedication to perfecting his craft, coupled with his innate charm, makes him a true stage master and a beloved presence in the group.

정호석

Jung Hoseok, born in early 1994, is the radiant sun of BTS. As the main dancer and a key rapper, his energy is the definition of infectious positivity. J-Hope is the member who meticulously ensures that the group's choreography is clean, powerful, and perfectly synchronized.

Off stage, his bright smile and optimistic outlook are a constant source of strength and hope for his bandmates and ARMY alike. He's incredibly hard-working and disciplined, but his fun-loving nature ensures he's a vital mood-maker who lifts everyone's spirits.

전정국

Jeon Jungkook, born in 1997, is the multi-talented youngest member, or maknae. Nicknamed the “Golden Maknae,” he excels in virtually everything he attempts: he is the main vocalist, a lead dancer, a sub-rapper, and the center of the group.

Beyond his on-stage roles, he's also skilled in drawing, video editing, photography, and even boxing. Jungkook's journey from a shy trainee to a global superstar is inspiring. He is characterized by his relentless work ethic, competitive spirit, and deep humility despite his immense talent.

민윤기

Min Yoongi, born in 1993, is the group's lead rapper and genius producer, known professionally as Suga and his alter-ego Agust D. He possesses a remarkably witty and sometimes intimidating persona, often contrasted by his deep capacity for warmth and his intense passion for music.

Yoongi is one of the most prolific members, having written and produced over a hundred songs for BTS and other artists. While he loves sleeping and a quiet life, his lyrics are sharp, deeply honest, and critical of society, reflecting his journey and inner struggles with incredible artistry.

김태형

Kim Taehyung, also born in 1995, is a vocalist and one of the group's visuals, instantly recognizable by his deep, baritone voice and striking good looks.

V is known for his unique, artistic sensibilities, which extend to his love of classic art, photography, and fashion. His personality is often described as quirky and free-spirited, bringing an unpredictable and charming energy to the group.

His stage performances are powerful and full of charismatic intensity, effortlessly switching between playful expressions and soulful depth. He is an imaginative soul who always encourages a fresh perspective.

김석진

Kim Seokjin, born in 1992, is the pillar of the group and holds the respected title of the oldest member, or hyung. Despite his status, he's famous for his hilarious “dad jokes,” easygoing nature, and his iconic “Worldwide Handsome” nickname. Jin's warm, supportive, and sometimes chaotic energy is essential for keeping the atmosphere light and positive.

Though he's the eldest, he often acts like the mischievous youngest, ensuring the team stays grounded, joyful, and well-fed! His beautiful, clear vocal tone adds a powerful emotional layer to every BTS track.
